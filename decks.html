Art Decks

Series & Folio Editions

Art Decks are the studio's printed folios — archival sheets gathered into a single body. Not cards. Not reproductions for the sake of selling. A series of works, held together as one piece.

Each edition is drawn from the different practices of the studio. Figurative and conceptual. Ink and surface. The line and the mark. Printed on archival stock, numbered, and kept as a complete set.

The Figurative Edition

Works from the monochrome study of the face and form.

The Surface Edition

Material and texture studies — ink, surface, and process.

The Empire Edition

A signature folio spanning the disciplines of the studio.

Archival sheets. Numbered. Kept as a set.
