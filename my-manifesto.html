My Manifesto

The Economy of Light: A Manifesto for the Hand

I

The Primacy of Value

I do not reject color. I simply understand that before color, there was value - the eternal struggle between the white of the paper and the black of the graphite. My monochrome practice is not a denial of hue. It is a return to the foundation. Value comes first. Everything else is built upon it.

II

The Tyranny of the Line

Representation is an act of discipline. To draw is to see, not merely to look. My work is representational because abstraction without foundation is a castle built on air. I demand that the eye recognizes the bone, the flesh, the stone, or the leaf. We must earn the right to distort by first proving we can replicate.

III

The Concept is the Soul

Technique is the vehicle, not the destination. A hyper-realistic drawing of an apple is merely a printer's trick if it does not ask a question. My art is conceptual because the pencil is a tool for thinking. Every stroke is a word; every composition is a sentence. If the drawing does not disturb the silence or provoke a thought, it is merely decoration.

IV

The Hand vs. The Machine

In an age of digital reproduction and artificial intelligence, the pencil is an act of resistance. It is the most primitive and honest of tools. There is no "undo" button in graphite. There is only the pressure of the hand, the tooth of the paper, and the smudge of the finger. The physical labor of hatching is a meditation.

V

The Liberation of Chroma

My color work is an explosion of energy. Surface design and graffiti are art that breathes. It moves with the body. It weathers with the city. It is touched, worn, passed by, and lived with. The line is the constant. The surface changes. The mark remains.

VI

The Aesthetic of Reduction

To strip away color is an act of mercy. It allows the viewer to feel the temperature of the subject. We render the world in ash and light to remind you that memory is rarely in color. Memory is in contrast.

This is my covenant with the paper and the wall.
