About the Artist

The Hands Behind the Work

I work in graphite and charcoal. I study faces. I chase the moment when a mark on paper stops being graphite and starts being someone you recognize — or someone you want to know.

My practice is built on a single conviction: that stripping away color reveals more than it hides.

"I don't draw faces. I draw what faces carry."

The Journey

2015Began drawing seriously. Portraits became an obsession.
2018Committed exclusively to monochrome.
2021First commissioned series. Presence over likeness.
2024Expanded into editions, AI integration, education.
2026Full studio established. Doors open worldwide.

The Philosophy

Art should hold the wall. It should command a room without shouting. Monochrome is not a limitation — it's a discipline.

This is not just a portfolio. It's the beginning of a body of work that will outlive me.
