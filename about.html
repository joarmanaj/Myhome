About the Artist

The Hands Behind the Work

I work in graphite and charcoal. I study faces. I chase the moment when a mark on paper stops being graphite and starts being someone you recognize — or someone you want to know.

My practice is built on a single conviction: that stripping away color reveals more than it hides.

"I don't draw faces. I draw what faces carry."
